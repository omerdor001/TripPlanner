# Trip Plan: Vienna (3 days)

_unverified — confirm hours/prices before travel_

**Interests:** art, food  
**Budget level:** mid-range  

## Vienna — Day 1
_All stops today are in the same area — easily walkable._

- **Cafe Sacher** (food, ~60 min, €12 (Cake and coffee combo))
  Historic Viennese coffeehouse famed for inventing the original Sacher-Torte, paired with classic coffeehouse ambiance.
  - Neighborhood: Innere Stadt
  - Best time to visit: Mid-afternoon
  - Opening hours: 08:00-22:00 daily
  - Booking recommended in advance
- **St. Stephen's Cathedral** (history, ~90 min, €6 (Basic entry; tower or catacombs cost extra))
  Iconic Gothic cathedral at the heart of Vienna with a striking tiled roof, catacombs, and panoramic tower views.
  - Neighborhood: Innere Stadt
  - Best time to visit: Early morning
  - Opening hours: Mon-Sat 09:00-11:30, 13:00-16:30
- **Hofburg Palace** (history, ~120 min, €16 (Combined ticket for Sisi Museum and apartments))
  Former imperial palace complex with opulent state apartments, the Sisi Museum, and the Imperial Treasury.
  - Neighborhood: Innere Stadt
  - Best time to visit: Morning
  - Opening hours: 09:00-17:30 daily
- **Albertina Museum** (art, ~120 min, €17 (Standard adult admission))
  Renowned museum with an extensive graphic arts collection plus Monet, Picasso, and Klimt works, housed in a former Habsburg residence.
  - Neighborhood: Innere Stadt
  - Best time to visit: Weekday afternoon
  - Opening hours: 10:00-18:00, Wed/Fri until 21:00
  - Booking recommended in advance

## Vienna — Day 2
_Today spans 2 areas (Museumsquartier, Mariahilf) — consider public transit or a taxi between stops._

- **Kunsthistorisches Museum** (art, ~150 min, €18 (Standard adult ticket, discounts for students/seniors))
  World-class art museum housing the Habsburgs' collection, including works by Bruegel, Vermeer, and Raphael, set in a lavish palatial building.
  - Neighborhood: Museumsquartier
  - Best time to visit: Weekday morning to avoid crowds
  - Opening hours: 10:00-18:00, closed Mondays (varies seasonally)
  - Booking recommended in advance
- **Leopold Museum** (art, ~120 min, €15 (Adult ticket price))
  Modern art museum famous for the world's largest Egon Schiele collection plus works by Klimt and other Austrian Expressionists.
  - Neighborhood: Museumsquartier
  - Best time to visit: Late afternoon
  - Opening hours: 10:00-18:00, Thu until 21:00
- **MuseumsQuartier Wien** (art, ~90 min, €0 (Free to wander the courtyards; individual museums charge entry))
  One of the largest cultural districts in the world, combining museums, courtyards, cafes, and public art installations in a relaxed setting.
  - Neighborhood: Museumsquartier
  - Best time to visit: Afternoon
  - Opening hours: Public spaces open daily
- **Naschmarkt** (food, ~120 min, €15 (Cost varies by what you eat and buy))
  Vienna's most famous market street, packed with food stalls, spice vendors, and casual eateries offering both Austrian and international cuisine.
  - Neighborhood: Mariahilf
  - Best time to visit: Saturday morning for the flea market
  - Opening hours: Mon-Sat 06:00-19:30 (varies by stall)

## Vienna — Day 3
_Today spans 3 areas (Innere Stadt, Landstraße, Leopoldstadt) — consider public transit or a taxi between stops._

- **Café Central** (food, ~75 min, €14 (Coffee and pastry))
  Grand historic cafe with soaring vaulted ceilings, once a haunt of Vienna's intellectuals, serving classic Viennese pastries and coffee.
  - Neighborhood: Innere Stadt
  - Best time to visit: Late morning to avoid lines
  - Opening hours: 07:30-22:00 daily
- **Figlmüller** (food, ~90 min, €25 (Main course with drink))
  Legendary restaurant credited with popularizing Vienna's giant schnitzel, a mid-range must-try dining spot.
  - Neighborhood: Innere Stadt
  - Best time to visit: Dinner, arrive early or book ahead
  - Opening hours: 11:30-23:00 daily
  - Booking recommended in advance
- **Belvedere Palace (Upper Belvedere)** (art, ~150 min, €21 (Timed entry ticket recommended))
  Baroque palace and gallery home to Klimt's 'The Kiss' and an extensive collection of Austrian art from medieval to contemporary.
  - Neighborhood: Landstraße
  - Best time to visit: Morning
  - Opening hours: 09:00-18:00 daily
  - Booking recommended in advance
- **Prater & Wurstelprater Food Stalls** (food, ~120 min, €20 (Includes Ferris wheel ride and street food))
  Historic amusement park area with the giant Ferris wheel, surrounded by traditional Austrian sausage stands and beer gardens.
  - Neighborhood: Leopoldstadt
  - Best time to visit: Early evening
  - Opening hours: Varies by ride/stall, generally 10:00-22:00
